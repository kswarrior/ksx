**KS Portal** is a fast, minimal, privacy-first browser built by **KS Warrior**. It keeps the interface out of your way so you can focus on the pages you care about.

## Why KS Portal?

- **Lightweight** — starts quickly and stays out of the way
- **Private by design** — no account required and no tracking
- **Powerful downloads** — pause, resume, rename and organize files
- **Tab groups** — keep related tabs together with names, icons and colors
- **Made yours** — custom backgrounds for the home screen, downloads and settings

## Browsing that feels simple

The start page puts your top sites, bookmarks and recent pages one tap away, and you can swap it for any custom home URL. Search from the address bar, use your own search engine, and open as many tabs as you need — with pinning, muting, duplicate cleanup, recently closed tabs and private tabs. **AI Mode** adds one-tap shortcuts to Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok, DeepSeek and DuckDuckGo AI.

## Privacy and security

- **Ad blocker** — curated rules work offline, with optional EasyList, EasyPrivacy, uBlock, Brave, AdGuard and URLhaus lists, plus custom filters and per-site exceptions
- **Safe Browsing** — warns about dangerous sites and downloads
- **Do Not Track** — sent with main page requests
- **Secure connections** — upgrades to HTTPS and asks before loading an insecure version
- **Incognito** — private tabs that leave no history behind
- **Site controls** — per-site permissions, JavaScript, cookies, pop-ups, media and notification settings, with automatic permission cleanup
- **On-device passwords** — save passwords and autofill forms; passkeys are never stored or intercepted

## Downloads and organization

Pause, resume and retry downloads, watch live speed and ETA, then sort, filter, rename, share or open files with another app. Bookmarks support folders and search, history has date filters and category cleanup, and site data can be reviewed or cleared per website.

## Tools and customization

Reader mode, save page offline, print or save as PDF, page translation, voice search, text size, desktop site, media autoplay controls and background media playback are built in. Dark mode for websites works on Android 10 and newer. Choose custom backgrounds, tune the toolbar, enable pull-to-refresh and edge-swipe gestures, and decide which sections appear on the start page.

## Install

1. Download the latest APK from this page.
2. Open the file and allow installs from your browser if Android asks.
3. Install KS Portal and start browsing.

KS Portal is developed in the open — [view the Android source on GitHub](https://github.com/kswarrior/opencode/tree/main/ks-apps/browser/android).
